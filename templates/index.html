How it works

No forms, no back-and-forth — just a booked call.

It behaves like a good receptionist: understands the request, checks the diary, confirms and follows up.

  1. 01

    Visitors ask in their own words

    “Next week, any afternoon” or “what’s the earliest you can do?” — the agent understands dates, times and follow-up questions.

  2. 02

    It checks the real calendar

    Only open slots are offered, and a slot taken by someone else a second ago is refused, so there are no double bookings.

  3. 03

    Confirmed, with a calendar invite

    The visitor gets a confirmation and an .ics invite; you get the booking and, when they ask about pricing or a demo, their contact details.
